# Vern Gambetta — Functional Path

## Who / what

Vern Gambetta: long-time athletic development coach. “Functional Path” is a principles framework for building athletes from the ground up rather than a single branded workout product.

## Core ideas to keep

- Have a roadmap: plan, execute, evaluate.
- Think big picture: complete athlete, not isolated qualities.
- Master fundamental movement skills before advanced sport skills.
- Learn to use the ground efficiently (gait as foundation).
- Train linkage: connect the whole kinetic chain (toenails to fingernails).
- Train the core as the center of action.
- Build sport-appropriate work capacity (SAID).
- Think long-term: training is cumulative.
- The individual is the focus.
