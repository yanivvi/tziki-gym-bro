# Dan John — Fundamental movements and simple strength

## Who / what

Dan John: throws coach and strength educator known for minimal, durable programming language. Best known for reducing training to a few human movements and simple, hard programs (*Intervention*, *Easy Strength*).

## Core ideas to keep

- Five basic human movements: **push, pull, hinge, squat, loaded carry**.
- Loaded carries are often the highest-leverage missing piece for general population.
- Simple is not easy: few movements, meaningful load, high frequency.
- “Same but different”: rotate variations within a pattern to progress without beating up joints.
- Hammer and stone (via McGill): build structure (stone) then express force (hammer).
- Programs that fit on an index card beat cluttered method-chasing.

## Product extraction

- Use the five movements as the **minimum viable pattern schema**; extend with lunge, rotate, corrective for completeness.
- Provide a “minimum effective program” generator: one exercise per pattern, 2–4 days/week.
- Promote carries in onboarding as a non-negotiable habit block.
- Variation engine: swap within pattern every 2 weeks (same-but-different).
