# Mike Boyle — Functional Training for Sports

## Who / what

Michael Boyle: long-time strength coach and popularizer of practical “functional” athletic training. Focus on what transfers to sport and durable athletes, with strong opinions on unilateral work and core anti-movement categories.

## Core ideas to keep

- Functional = purposeful for the demands of the activity, not circus-balance theater.
- Prefer unilateral lower-body strength progressions (e.g. rear-foot-elevated split squat family).
- Posterior chain strength and power are distinct; train both (heavy hinge/trap-bar strength vs jumps/sprints/power).
- Core as anti-extension, anti-lateral flexion, anti-rotation — not endless sit-ups.
- Warm-up / activation can include “corrective-like” work without calling everything rehab.
- Hang Olympic variations (hang clean / hang snatch) as power tools when coaching quality exists.
- Trap-bar deadlift as a primary bilateral max-strength hinge option in many Boyle-influenced templates.
