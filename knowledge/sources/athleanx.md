# Athlean-X / Jeff Cavaliere

## Who / what

Jeff Cavaliere (MSPT, CSCS): physical therapist and strength coach; former Head PT for the New York Mets. ATHLEAN-X blends athletic conditioning, hypertrophy, and injury-aware exercise selection for a mass consumer audience.

## Core ideas to keep

- Train like an athlete: look and perform athletic, not bodybuilding-only.
- Movement patterns over body-part isolation as the primary program skeleton.
- Biomechanics and fiber direction matter (muscle-marker teaching style).
- Prefer safer alternatives when they achieve the same goal.
- Correctives and postural work (e.g. face pulls, scapular control) belong in normal training, not only rehab.
- Progressive overload on compounds remains the strength foundation.

## Movement pattern set (consumer-facing)

Athlean materials commonly organize full-body work around:

1. Squat
2. Lunge
3. Hinge
4. Push (horizontal and/or vertical)
5. Pull
6. Carry
7. Corrective

## Product extraction

- Use as the **voice and cueing** layer for general users.
- Use 7-pattern checklist to validate that a generated workout is complete.
- Borrow substitution logic: if an exercise is high-risk for a common issue, offer a safer pattern-matched swap.
- Do not treat paid program day-plans as open content to reproduce.

## Notable programming notes

- WARRIOR example split evolution: Upper/Lower → PPL → Total Body across months.
- Emphasis on ballistic / athletic power mixed with hypertrophy methods (effective reps, lengthened partials, TUT) in advanced programs.
- Beginner messaging: full ROM, commandable loads, compounds first, do not ignore accessories that fix posture and joint health.
