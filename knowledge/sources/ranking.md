# Source ranking for a gym-trainer app

Ranked by usefulness for building product logic (not by brand popularity).

Scoring lens: pattern clarity, programmability, safety defaults, consumer accessibility, assessment-to-action loop.

| Tier | Source | Best use in product | Caveat |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | Dan John (5 movements + Intervention / Easy Strength) | Core movement taxonomy; simple program templates; carry emphasis | Light on formal screening |
| **1** | Athlean-X / Jeff Cavaliere | Consumer-facing exercise selection, correctives, form cues, athletic hypertrophy framing | Paid programs proprietary; YouTube is concept-rich, not a full periodization engine |
| **1** | Gray Cook / FMS | Assessment gates, corrective priority order, pattern before parts | Full FMS is certified IP; use principles, not unlicensed screen scoring |
| **1** | Stuart McGill | Spine-sparing core defaults, stage progression, stone then hammer | Clinical depth; keep app UX simple (bracing, endurance Big 3 style) |
| **2** | Mike Boyle | Unilateral lower-body progressions, anti-core categories, athletic templates | More coach/athlete than general consumer |
| **2** | EXOS | Session structure (pillar, move prep, strength, ESD, regen); Position-Pattern-Power | Certification-heavy; distill structure only |
| **2** | Juan Carlos Santana | Equipment menus, sport templates, exercise catalogs | Broad catalog; needs filtering for app scope |
| **3** | Vern Gambetta | Philosophy / long-term athletic development principles | Less plug-and-play exercise programming |
| **3** | DNS | Deep motor-control / developmental stability | Too clinical for v1 consumer app unless optional advanced layer |

## Recommended adoption order for this project

1. Adopt unified patterns from Dan John + Athlean-X (push, pull, hinge, squat, lunge, carry, rotate, corrective).
2. Adopt hierarchy from Cook + McGill (mobility, stability, pattern, load, power/speed).
3. Adopt session skeleton from EXOS-lite (prep, main strength/power, conditioning, finish/corrective).
4. Adopt exercise bias from Boyle (unilateral lower body, anti-rotation core, posterior chain).
5. Use Athlean-X-style consumer cues for explanations and injury-aware substitutions.
6. Keep DNS / full FMS as later pro or coach-mode features.

## What not to copy wholesale

- Proprietary Athlean-X workout schedules or meal plans as product content.
- Official FMS scoring sheets or certification materials as a branded feature without rights.
- Medical diagnosis claims from McGill/DNS content.
