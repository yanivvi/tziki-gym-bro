# DNS — Dynamic Neuromuscular Stabilization

## Who / what

DNS (Prague School, Prof. Pavel Kolar et al.): clinical and performance approach based on developmental kinesiology. Uses developmental postures and reflex locomotion concepts to restore joint centration and ideal stabilization strategies.

## Core ideas to keep

- Ideal stabilization and locomotion patterns are encoded in early development.
- Adult training/rehab can re-access those patterns via developmental positions and support-zone stimulation.
- Goal: subconscious proper stability pattern, then cortical ownership via exercise, then performance loading.
- Used in athletic rehab and high-level sport contexts; broad course tracks (sport, ortho, women’s health, etc.).
