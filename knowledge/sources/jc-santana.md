# Juan Carlos Santana — Functional Training (IHP)

## Who / what

Juan Carlos Santana: founder of Institute of Human Performance (IHP), early popularizer of functional training alongside peers like Gambetta, Gary Gray, Paul Chek. Author of *Functional Training* (Human Kinetics).

## Core ideas to keep

- Functional training as a bridge between sport demands and traditional lifting.
- Broad tools: bodyweight, bands/pulleys, dumbbells/kettlebells, medicine balls, stability balls.
- Program across strength, endurance, power, and sport-specific needs.
- Large exercise catalog with progressions and sequencing.
